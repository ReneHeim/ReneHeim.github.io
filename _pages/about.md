I am an interdisciplinary researcher and team builder at the interface of **plant pathology, plant ecology, geography, data science and remote sensing**. Trained in bioengineering ([HAW Hamburg](https://www.haw-hamburg.de/bachelor-biotechnologie)) and plant ecology ([Universität Hamburg](https://www.biologie.uni-hamburg.de/en/einrichtungen/ipm.html)), I hold a joint PhD in remote sensing of plant diseases from [Macquarie University](https://www.mq.edu.au/) and Universität Hamburg.

I study how **biotic and abiotic stress modulates plant functional traits**, such as leaf angle, leaf area and leaf structure, and therefore the **remote sensing signals** that drone and satellite sensors detect, **from leaf to landscape**.

Because models are only as good as their reference data, I build **open and transparent research software and data**. I bring together **interdisciplinary teams** from plant science, geography and data science to tackle these questions jointly.

I work at the University of Göttingen and co-founded the Plant Health Remote Sensing Lab at the [University of Pretoria](https://www.up.ac.za/fabi). Students interested in these topics are welcome to [get in touch](/contact/).
